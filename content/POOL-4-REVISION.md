Heavy Girder’s extra Gold is once per physical copy per round. It checks actual placement Gold earned by another copy earlier in the round, including copies no longer on the board. A replay can earn an as-yet-unclaimed conditional bonus once it qualifies, but cannot repeat either payment. Flair Gold and the wallet do not qualify.

Bounce history includes Construct and animal abilities, lasts only for the current round, and powers Seasoned Framework’s enhanced Support. Bounce immunity applies to both sources, including Flair. Closing Arch gains +12 instead of +8 after earlier Support; Tempered Column gains +4 once, even with two Reinforced neighbours.
